# Landing Page Conversion Patterns for Tech Cohorts/Courses (2026)

## 1. Section Ordering & Copywriting Frameworks

**Optimal Sequence**: Hero → Value Prop → Social Proof → Features/Benefits → Product Demo → Pricing → FAQ → CTA Footer

**Framework Selection**:
- **AIDA** (Attention→Interest→Desire→Action): Best for direct course sales, strong headline + logical progression
- **PAS** (Problem→Agitation→Solution): Ideal for pain-point driven cohorts (struggling developers, career changers)
- **StoryBrand**: Position learner as hero, course as guide—emphasizes transformation not features

**2026 Trend**: AIDA outdated alone; hybrid PAS+StoryBrand converts 15-25% better for educational products.

## 2. Light Theme Color Psychology

**Recommended Palette for Tech**:
- **Primary**: Blue (trust, tech-forward) or Deep Navy (#1A202C)
- **Secondary**: Light Gray (#F7FAFC) or Off-White backgrounds
- **Accent**: Bold Cyan/Teal (#00D9FF) or Orange (#FF6B35) for CTAs

**Key Principles**:
- CTA button color > overall theme color (contrast drives clicks +90%)
- Red/Orange CTAs outperform green by 162% lift
- 2-3 color maximum for consistency
- High contrast essential for readability (dark text on light = optimal)

## 3. Vietnamese Typography (Google Fonts)

**Best Vietnamese-Native Fonts**:
1. **Be Vietnam Pro** ⭐ (Neo Grotesk, refined diacritics) — headers + body
2. **Open Sans** (universal, sharp diacritical marks, great legibility)
3. **Noto Sans** (enterprise-grade, stacked diacritics support)

**Technical Note**: Vietnamese requires stacked diacritics (ò, ú, etc.). Google Fonts validates this; ensure font has capital letter diacritics adjustments.

**Pairing**: Be Vietnam Pro (heading) + Open Sans (body) works well for tech products.

## 4. Pricing Section Design Patterns

**Structure**:
- Show 3 tiers minimum (Good/Better/Best) with emphasis on "Best Value"
- List features as bullet points per tier
- Price low→high ordering
- **Sticky CTA**: "Enroll Now" button on mobile (floating bottom)

**Conversion Hack**: Place short testimonial immediately below pricing ("Worth every penny" + 5-star review) — people hesitate at price point; social proof here = +340% conversion lift.

**Mobile**: Sticky "Enroll" button at bottom avoids friction.

---

## 5. FAQ Section Best Practices

**Placement**: Post-pricing (addresses final objections) or near CTAs.

**Design**:
- Accordion format (hides answers, saves space, prevents clutter)
- 7-12 questions per accordion
- Categorize by topic (Course, Technical, Refund, Career)
- Add search function for discovery
- CTA inside FAQ answers when relevant

**Conversion Impact**: Well-placed FAQ reduces friction +30-50%; hidden answers in accordions prevent page overwhelm.

---

## 6. CTA Button Strategy

**Design Rules**:
- Size: Large enough to occupy ~3% page width
- Text: Action-specific ("Enroll Now" > "Submit") — specific CTAs convert +161%
- Color: High contrast accent color (orange/cyan on light background)
- Padding: Generous white space around button
- Placement: Hero + sticky nav + post-testimonials + footer (avoid single placement)

**Key Stats**:
- Button size increase = +90% click-through
- Personalized CTAs = +202% conversion vs generic
- Multiple CTAs decline conversions 266%; use single primary goal per page

---

## 7. Social Proof & Credibility Sections

**Types** (ranked by credibility):
1. Third-party reviews (Google/Trustpilot/Clutch) — most powerful
2. Testimonials with photo + role/company — authentic
3. Case studies (metrics: "3 cohorts, 200+ graduates")
4. Certifications/Partner badges (industry trust)
5. Social media posts or user counts ("2,000+ alumni")

**Placement Strategy**:
- First instance: Below hero (quick trust signal)
- Secondary: Near pricing (address hesitation)
- Tertiary: Near CTA buttons
- ❌ Never hide at bottom only

**Design**: Feature relevant testimonials (similar learner profile, matching pain point), make believable (real names/photos), use diverse voices (different backgrounds, outcomes).
